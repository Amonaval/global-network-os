The $100M RoadmapStep-by-step from today — if I were you, starting now
Stage 0
Stop Building. Start Depending.
Weeks 1–4
Every morning before writing a single line of code, open Knowledge Hub and use it for the current engineering work. Not to test it. To depend on it.

At the end of 30 days, answer honestly: Did I open Knowledge Hub before starting any engineering session this week without being reminded? Is there one specific thing I found that I would not have remembered otherwise? Would I notice if it was gone tomorrow?

If any answer is no — identify what is missing and deepen what exists. Not a new feature. Deepen the gap map, sharpen the IDS explanation, make the decision archaeology surface things you actually forgot.
Gate: All three questions answered "yes" for two consecutive weeks = Stage 0 complete.
Stage 1
Find Five Aaravs
Weeks 4–10
Not engineering teams — they evaluate skeptically, prefer open source, and have procurement cycles. Go to independent IT consultants in India (Aarav persona). They have NDAs, cannot use cloud AI, already run Ollama, buy with their own card in 5 minutes.

Where to find them: LinkedIn (search "independent IT consultant India" → send 20 direct messages: "Do you ever spend 3 weeks solving a problem you already solved at a different client because you can't find your notes?"), NASSCOM freelancer communities, r/IndiaITprofessionals.

What to do with them: give the product free, watch them use it over a Zoom call. Say nothing. Watch where they get confused, what they skip, what makes them say "oh, that's useful." That session is worth more than 3 months of building.
Target: 5 Aaravs actively using the product within 6 weeks of Stage 0 complete.
Stage 2
The First Payment
Weeks 8–14
Charge the first Aarav who says "this saved me time" or "I actually found something I was looking for." Not because you need the money. Because a paid user gives honest feedback. A free user just stops logging in.

The price: ₹1,500/month (~$18). The conversation: "I'm glad this is helping. I'm starting to charge for it — ₹1,500/month. Want to keep going?"

If they say no: ask why. That answer is the most valuable thing you will hear this year.

Do not build a payment system yet. Take the first payment via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Razorpay link. Build proper billing at 10 paying users.
Target: 1 paying user within 10 weeks of Stage 1 start. This is not a revenue milestone — it is a validation milestone.
Stage 3
Choose the Vertical
Months 3–5
By this point you have talked to 20–30 people. One group has the sharpest pain and the fastest "I need this" moment. That group is your first vertical.

Strong prediction: Chartered Accountants (CAs), India-first. 1.8M registered, every cloud AI tool violates client confidentiality, VIDUR AI is cloud-only and law-database-only, ICAI has strong community infrastructure, ₹1,500/month is less than one billable hour.

What "choose the vertical" means in practice: Change onboarding prompts to CA-specific questions ("Upload your client files and tax filings. Write one article: "I built a private knowledge base for CA practices — runs on your laptop, no cloud, your data never leaves." Post in CA LinkedIn groups and ICAI forums. This takes 2 days to build with AI assistance.
Target: 10 paying users from one specific profession by Month 5.
Stage 4
Community-Led Growth
Months 4–8

The Critical WarningsThe traps that kill products like this — named specifically
🚨
Warning 1: The Feature Trap

The product has 20+ intelligence features. Most users will use 3–4 of them daily. Building more features before knowing which 3–4 those are is the most expensive mistake you can make. After Stage 1, the next 6 months should deepen the features that prove indispensable — not add new ones. Every feature built without a paying user asking for it is inventory that may never ship.

⚠️
Warning 2: The Engineering Team Trap

Engineering teams feel like the obvious first customer because you are one. They are not. They evaluate tools skeptically, prefer open source, have long procurement cycles, and will consider building AnythingLLM internally. Aarav (IT consultant) and the CA convert faster and generate better signal faster. Get your first 50 paying users from individual professionals before going after engineering teams.

⚠️
Warning 3: The Premature Enterprise Trap

Enterprise feels like the biggest prize. It is — eventually. Before $500K ARR, enterprise sales cycles (3–9 months) consume all your time and energy for small returns. Get to $500K ARR from individuals and small firms first. Enterprise comes to you when they hear about you from the individuals already using it inside their organization.

💡
Warning 4: The Tool Identity Trap

The moment a journalist or analyst asks "what is Knowledge Hub?" you must have one sentence that needs no follow-up. Not "it's like Notion but local" or "it's like Cursor but for knowledge." The answer: "Knowledge Hub is the first Professional Organizational Memory platform. It makes your accumulated knowledge compound instead of decay, privately." Practice saying this until it's natural and requires no explanation.

✓
What AI Acceleration Actually Changes (And Doesn't)

AI compresses your build timeline 10×. New vertical onboarding: 2 days. Enterprise SSO: 3 days. Second vertical spin-up: 3–5 days. What AI does NOT change: the 30 days required to validate daily irreplaceability. The 6 weeks for Aarav to trust new software with client files. The 12–18 months to prove a vertical works. Distribution, trust, and validation are human-speed problems. Use AI acceleration for vertical expansion — not for building more features before validating the ones that exist.

The company worth $100M is already inside the product that exists.
The only question is when you introduce it to its first paying customer.
Go find that person this week. Not next month. This week. Everything else on this roadmap follows from that.
