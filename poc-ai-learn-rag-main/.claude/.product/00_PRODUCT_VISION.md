# Product Vision

## Mission

Transform how professionals and organizations turn accumulated knowledge into active intelligence — starting with engineering teams, expanding to every profession where that knowledge compounds privately.

## North Star Metric

**Intelligence Density Score (IDS) per organization.**
IDS > 0.7 = customers never leave. Every product decision must optimize the speed at which customers reach IDS > 0.7.

## First Principle

> Customer data belongs to the customer. Intelligence generated from that data belongs to the platform.

The intelligence layer — query memory, decision archaeology, gap evolution, risk history, onboarding paths — is generated from behavior, not documents. It cannot be reconstructed by any competitor. It is the MOAT.

## Where We Compete

**Engineering organizational memory.** No mainstream product owns this category. Not Notion, not Confluence AI, not Linear, not GitHub Copilot. The accumulated intelligence layer — query memory, decision archaeology, gap evolution, risk history, health trending — is a standalone product category that does not yet exist at commercial quality.

**Professional knowledge intelligence for regulated industries.** Doctors, lawyers, financial advisors, consultants, and researchers cannot legally or ethically send their work files to cloud AI. For them, local-first is the only option — not a preference. No mainstream AI product competes in this space. This is a second category we own, with the same compounding intelligence model and the same 90-day moat.

## Where We Do Not Compete

**Code generation quality** — Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Windsurf own this loop.
**IDE-native integration** — same players, deeply entrenched.
**Real-time collaboration** — Notion, Linear, GitHub own this.
**General document search** — Glean, Notion AI, Confluence AI own this.

Our code generation capability exists as a feature that leverages our intelligence layer — not as a standalone product competing in those categories.

## Differentiators

- Local-first, privacy-first (the only architecture enterprise engineering teams will trust with internal decisions)
- Intelligence compounds with usage (switching cost increases every week — the 90-day moat)
- Cross-source knowledge synthesis (docs + decisions + memory + gaps in one view)
- Proactive intelligence (the platform watches the repo — it doesn't wait to be asked)

## Milestones (Stage-Gated, Not Time-Boxed)

**Stage 1 — Irreplaceability**
Builder uses the product every day on real engineering work.
Would notice if it was gone tomorrow.
At least one intelligence signal is genuinely relied upon, not just consulted.

**Stage 2 — Five People**
Five engineers with identical pain use it and love it.
Each can articulate the value in one sentence without being prompted.
Five people who love it is worth more than fifty who are mildly interested.

**Stage 3 — First Charge**
At least one person pays before the product is "ready."
Payment is a validation milestone, not a revenue milestone.
Paying customers give honest feedback. Free users stop using it silently.

**Stage 4 — Feature Requests Replace Feature Ideas**
Paying customers ask for specific things repeatedly.
The roadmap is driven by requests, not imagination.
Scale begins here. Segments: engineering teams (10–500), consulting firms, regulated industries, individual developers.
