# Customer Strategy

*The philosophy behind who we serve, in what order, and why.*

---

## The Core Insight

Every professional accumulates knowledge over their career. Most of it becomes inaccessible.
It sits in folders, PDFs, emails, notes, and the heads of people who have since moved on.

The problem is not storage. The problem is that accumulated knowledge stops compounding the
moment it becomes unsearchable, unqueryable, and disconnected from the work happening today.

Knowledge Hub turns accumulated knowledge into active intelligence.
That is a universal problem. It is not specific to software engineers.

---

## The Two Unlocks

**Unlock 1: Privacy.**
Most professionals cannot use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini with their actual work files.
Doctors have HIPAA. Lawyers have attorney-client privilege. Consultants have NDAs.
Financial advisors have fiduciary duty. Researchers have pre-publication agreements.

For these users, cloud AI is not a cost decision — it is a compliance and ethics decision.
Local-first is not our limitation. It is their only option.
We are the only intelligence platform they can actually use.

**Unlock 2: Cheap local LLMs.**
Ollama has made running a capable LLM free and local. Most professionals don't know this.
The intelligence layer on top — which is what makes the LLM useful on *their specific data* —
is what we provide. They bring the documents. We provide the intelligence engine.
The LLM is a commodity. The intelligence layer is not.

---

## Deployment Spectrum

We are not a single deployment model. We are an intelligence layer that runs on any model,
hosted any way the customer needs.

```
Fully Private          ←————————————————→         Fully Cloud
(user's machine)                                   (our servers)

Local-First      Self-Hosted      KH Managed      Pay-Per-Query
Free LLM         Own Cloud        Isolated         Shared Infra
                 Private VPC      Instance         Cheapest
```

The customer chooses where they sit on this spectrum based on:
- How sensitive their data is
- How technical they are
- How much they're willing to pay
- How much infrastructure they want to manage

We do not force them onto one model. We serve across the spectrum.

---

## How We Sequence

We do not target everyone. We target in order of:

1. **Pain intensity** — the more painful the current situation, the faster they adopt
2. **Privacy requirement** — if cloud AI is not an option, we are not competing with it
3. **Willingness to pay** — professionals with business budgets pay more reliably than individuals
4. **IDS velocity** — how fast does their intelligence compound? (team > solo; daily queries > weekly)
5. **Referral density** — who talks to others like them? (professional networks, Slack communities)

The highest-value early customer: someone with strong privacy requirements, daily knowledge work,
and a professional budget who currently has no viable alternative.

---

## The Market Framing

We are not a "documentation search tool."
We are not a "RAG platform."
We are not a "code intelligence tool."

We are the intelligence layer for private knowledge.

Any professional or organization that:
- Has accumulated documents, decisions, and notes over years
- Cannot (or will not) send that data to cloud AI
- Needs to query, synthesize, and build on that knowledge continuously

— is a Knowledge Hub customer.

The software engineering use case is where we started because it is closest to the builder.
It is not where we end.
