Pre-Meeting Brief
Day-before read
Intelligence OS Demo 30 minutes Internal coffee chat
What you're showing

A pattern for giving any Claude session — individual or org-wide — persistent institutional memory. Company constitution, product vision, executive council perspectives, active missions, decision records. All plain Markdown files; no infrastructure. The Syndigo version is pre-populated with real context and ready to demo live.

Five moments to nail
  • 01Defense test — Propose a feature already in the decision records as rejected. Claude cites the record and the reasoning. Don't explain it first — let the moment land.
  • 02Session restart — Close entirely. Type "start". Full company context restored. Conversation is disposable; the OS is not.
  • 03Alignment check — Ask: "Are our active missions still aligned with the company constitution and the direction leadership has set?" Watch it synthesize an honest answer.
  • 04Four-level hierarchy — Company → Division → Team → Personal. Each inherits the layer above automatically. A developer session carries the full company baseline without thinking about it.
  • 05The close — "AI makes individuals productive. The Intelligence OS makes the organization intelligent. That's a different order of magnitude."
If only one moment lands
The defense test. Lead with it — don't build up to it. It's the most surprising thing in the demo and it earns the next 25 minutes.
The line to leave them with
"Stop prompting. Start compounding."
