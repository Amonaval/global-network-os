Working public product profile · August 2026

Private networks that can trust, discover and federate without becoming public social graphs.

TrustWeave is a governed Network OS for families, alumni, professional groups, businesses, franchises and communities. People can belong to many private networks; networks can build trusted peer bridges; and networks can affiliate upward into communities, chapters and federations while exposing only deliberately shared profiles.

The cross-shaped product model

Two dimensions create the Network OS.

The product is easiest to understand as a cross: horizontal trust connects independently governed peer networks; vertical federation lets smaller networks belong to larger umbrellas. The person remains governed by each local network context.

Horizontal dimension

Network ↔ Network trust

Accepted bridges create permissioned reach between peer networks. Discovery can stay anonymous until a consented introduction reveals identity.

Application layer

One governed trust structure, many products.

Applications reuse identity, affiliation, privacy, launch control and consent instead of rebuilding trust for every use case.

Trusted-community matrimony

A candidate does not start as an unverified stranger profile. A person can be linked to a claimed family identity; that family can have a verified community affiliation; discovery can expose only opted-in matrimony fields; identity/contact reveal can remain mutual-consent based.

Claimed person→Family→Verified community→Opt-in profile→Mutual introduction

Expertise, mentorship, referrals & jobs

Ask for a Navy officer, architect, doctor, CA, mentor or job referral across trusted networks. Search can remain anonymous until a target accepts an introduction.

Business discovery with provenance

Find suppliers, accountants, service providers or investors through a community/association graph where the source network and trust path provide context beyond a public listing.

Community operating layer

Events, relocation help, emergency coordination, senior support, youth groups, education assistance, donations and verified directories can share the same governed identity foundation.

Franchise and distributed business federation

Local stores/outlets remain independent operational networks while affiliating to city, region and national umbrellas. Public business profile and internal operating graph stay distinct.

Privacy boundary

Federation is not graph merging.

Private by default

Exact family relationships, private memories, contact information, internal organization details, private photos and governed history stay inside the source network.

Explicitly federated

Network name, origin, broad locations, verified affiliations, selected expertise, opted-in people, coordinator roles and application-specific public fields can be shared under policy.

Private graph→Publication policy→Federated profile→Umbrella app
