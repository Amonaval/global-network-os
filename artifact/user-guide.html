Hierarchy Network

Your family's story, all in one place

See how everyone is connected — from grandparents to grandchildren — on a living, interactive family tree anyone can explore.

No technical knowledge needed
What this app does

Four things, done simply

The app has four views. You don't need to use all of them — start with the tree and explore from there.

Family Tree

A visual diagram showing parents, children, spouses, and siblings — solid lines for family, dashed lines for spouses.

Member Directory

Browse everyone alphabetically. Filter by profession or city. Tap any card to see a full profile.

Location Map

See where family members live around the world. Dot size shows how many people are in each city.

Your Profile

Submit your name, photo, profession, city, and a short bio. The family admin reviews and publishes it.

Getting started

How to add yourself to the tree

You don't need a login to submit your profile. Just fill in a short form and the family admin will add you.

1

Open the app and tap "Submit Profile"

Find the Submit Profile button in the top-right corner of any page. Tap it to open the profile form. This is open to everyone — no account needed.

2

Fill in your details

The only required field is your full name. Everything else — profession, city, date of birth, photo, and a short bio — is optional but helps family members recognise you.

For your photo, paste a link to an image (e.g. from Google Photos or a shared drive). Direct photo upload is coming soon.
3

Submit and wait for approval

Tap Submit. Your profile goes to the family admin for review. Once approved, you'll appear in the tree, directory, and map automatically.

4

Your connections are added by the admin

After your profile is approved, the admin links you to your parents, spouse, and children in the tree. If something looks wrong — a missing connection, a wrong name — let the admin know to fix it.

Tell the admin: your parents' names, your spouse's name, and your children's names. This is how they build the connections.
Navigating the app

Three ways to explore your family

Use the left sidebar (or the menu on mobile) to switch between views at any time.

Hierarchy Tree

The main view. Every person is a card. Solid lines connect parents and children; dashed lines connect spouses. Click any card to see that person's full profile and family connections. Use the zoom controls (bottom-left) to zoom in and out. Use the minimap (bottom-right) to jump to a different part of a large tree.

Tip: click "Focus Lineage" on any person to show only their branch

Member Directory

A searchable list of everyone in the tree. Type a name, profession, or city in the search box. Use the dropdowns to filter by profession or location. Tap View Profile on any card for full details, or Focus Lineage to jump to their branch in the tree.

Tap "Submit Profile" here to add yourself

Location Map

A world map with dots where family members live. Larger dots mean more people in that city. Click a dot to see who lives there. Only the city is shown — not home addresses — so everyone's privacy is protected.

Only city-level location, never exact addresses
Profile details

What you'll see on someone's profile

Click any person in the tree or directory to open their profile panel on the right side of the screen.

i

Name, generation & photo

At the top you'll see their photo (or initials), full name, and which generation they belong to. Generation 1 is the oldest on the tree; higher numbers are younger.

i

Work, location & dates

Profession, city, country, and date of birth. If the person has passed away, you'll see In memoriam on their card in the tree — their profile and connections are fully preserved.

i

Family connections

A list of all linked relatives — parents, children, and spouse — each with a button to jump directly to their profile. Use this to navigate the tree person by person.

Tap "View in Family Tree" at the bottom of any profile to jump straight to that person in the tree diagram.
Helpful tips

Things worth knowing

On a phone? The tree works best on a wider screen, but you can pinch-to-zoom and scroll. The directory and map work great on any screen size.
Can't find someone? They may not have been added yet. Ask the family admin or submit a profile request on their behalf — just put their name and what you know.
In memoriam members appear slightly greyed out in the tree. You can toggle them on or off using the "Hide deceased / Show deceased" button above the tree.
Lineage focus is the most useful feature. Find someone in the directory, tap "Focus Lineage", and the tree zooms in to show only their ancestors, descendants, and siblings.
Something wrong? Profile details, missing connections, or incorrect information can all be fixed by the family admin. Reach out to them directly — they can edit any record.
Your privacy. Contact details (phone, email) are visible only to signed-in members, never to anyone browsing without an account. The map shows only your city, not your address.
